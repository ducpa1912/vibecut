# Loop 17 — founder test

1. Open **Edit** and the **Stories** tab. Press **Write a story**.
2. **Must see:** a thesis bar with **No model · basic story** (unless Apple Intelligence is on),
   the beat count and runtime, and one source line per beat.
3. Read the beat lines. **Must see:** every line is something that is actually said in the video.
4. Open **Preview** and press **Play story order**. **Must see:** the beats play in story order, not
   source order.
5. Press **Write again**. **Must see:** another story is created; the old one stays in the edit menu.
