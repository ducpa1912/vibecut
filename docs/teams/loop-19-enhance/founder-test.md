# Loop 19 — founder test

1. Open a **360p** project, **Edit → Cut**. Find **Enhance**.
   **Must see:** `640×360 → 1920×1080`, a **Lanczos upscale** switch and a **Sharpen** slider.
2. Turn the switch on, set Sharpen to 60.
   **Must see:** “No new detail: upscaling cannot recover what the source never captured.”
3. Reload. **Must see:** the switch and the slider stay.
4. Open a **1080p** project's Cut tab. **Must see:** no controls, and the line says no upscale is
   added.
