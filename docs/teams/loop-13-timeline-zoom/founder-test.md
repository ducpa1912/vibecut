# Loop 13 — founder test

1. Open **Edit** and look at the timeline head.
2. Press **+** a few times. **Must see:** the beats and scenes widen, the ruler shows more labels,
   and the rest of the page does not scroll sideways.
3. Click a scene block far from the playhead. **Must see:** that scene is selected and the player
   jumps to it; the playhead stays in view.
4. Press **−** and **Fit**. **Must see:** the timeline returns to the whole video at 1×.
