# Loop 14 — founder test

1. Open **Edit** and choose the **Cut** tab.
2. Press **9:16**. **Must see:** the picture becomes a tall 1080×1920 frame and Fill is selected.
3. Press **1:1** and **4:5**. **Must see:** the output label and the picture change to match.
4. Set Crop H to 0.6, press **Apply crop**. **Must see:** the picture keeps only that band.
5. Press **Reset crop**; press **Fit**. **Must see:** the whole source is back inside the frame.
6. Find **Warm / Cool / B&W / Vivid** in **Cut → Look**. They are no longer in SFX.
7. Return to **Source** and confirm the picture is the original aspect again.
