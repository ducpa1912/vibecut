# Loop 11 — founder test

1. Open **Preview**. Nothing should start on its own; the card says **No proof yet**.
2. Press **Prove this scene · blur**. The picture returns in about a second and the text is blurred,
   not rebuilt.
3. The strip on the picture reads **1 · Detect / 2 · Compare / 3 · Review**.
   - **Detect** shows boxes on the lines the proof will blur.
   - **Compare** is the split slider.
   - **Review** is the blurred frame.
4. Change **Fill / Blur / Off** in Edit or Clean. **Must see:** no proof starts by itself.
5. Press **Re-clean this scene**. **Must see:** Clean Focus opens on that scene.
6. In Clean Focus, change **X / Y / W / H** and press **Apply box**, then **Prove this scene**.
   **Must see:** the box and the blur move together.
7. Press **Re-check & re-apply**. **Must see:** the box you set is kept.
