# VibeCut QA: short-form repurposer persona (port 8801)

How I tested: I used browser tab-3 against http://localhost:8801. I did not tick the rights checkbox and did not attest. The Import button stays disabled without that box, so I imported both test videos through `POST /api/jobs/path`, the same call the Path tab makes. The server accepted both without any attestation. I did everything after that in the Studio UI, with a few API and data-dir reads to confirm what I saw. I changed no repo files.

Timings (wall clock on this Mac):
- **Reading (import).** A (53 s) was Ready in about 1 min. B (89 s) was Ready about 6.4 min after import, queued behind A. Jobs read one at a time.
- **Check.** A took 24.2 s. B took 13.7 s.
- **Find stories.** Under 2 s. **Build captions:** about 1 s.
- **Final 9:16 render.** A story 2 (26 s clip) took 2 min 50 s. Progress sat at 50% the whole time. A story 1 failed after 16 s.
- **Draft render.** 9 to 15 s per story, output 540x960.

## Persona verdict

Would I pay? **Not yet.** I would make 0 clips per hour today:
- I can't import in the UI without stating I own the footage.
- I can't cover old subtitles.
- Captions I build crash both the preview and the final render.
- When a render does work, I can't play or download it from Finish.
- The publish kit returns 400.

Check is quick (about 25 s), story finding is instant, draft renders are fast (9 to 15 s), and the colour presets are fine. The core repurpose loop is still broken.

Top 3 asks:
1. **Fix caption render (`\n` crash) and draw captions and text in the playing preview.** I need to see what I'll get, live.
2. **One-click "cover old subtitles" band**, auto-placed from Check, with new captions moved above it. (The rights policy is the owner's call, but without this the tool doesn't fit my workflow.)
3. **Real batch:** many short stories (15/30 s presets), Apply-to-all that actually builds captions everywhere, a faster Final render with ETA, and Download / Download-all on every finished cut.
